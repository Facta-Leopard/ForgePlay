FORGEPLAY VR × FORGEPLAYVP

Your Mac’s VR games, on Vision Pro.

ForgePlay on Mac runs compatible Windows VR games. ForgePlayVP on Vision Pro connects the game view with head and hand input. The experience uses both devices.

See ForgePlay VR in action.

Watch Dagon running through a Mac and Vision Pro. Then explore the connection flow and settings in the interactive guide above.

Point and click with your hands.

Point at a target and pinch your thumb and index finger to select it, without a separate VR controller. Compatible games can also combine this with gamepad input. Available controls depend on the game’s input support and bindings.
